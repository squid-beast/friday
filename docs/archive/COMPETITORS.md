# COMPETITOR RESEARCH — who else is building a "Jarvis" (Aug 2026)

## 1. HeyClicky (YC-backed, heyclicky.com)
What it is: Mac-native AI buddy that lives on-screen. Hotkey-invoked; sees your screen
"on demand" (screenshot only when asked), voice in real time, draws arrows/highlights on
screen, spawns background agents, integrates Gmail/Notion.
Pricing: free tier -> $20/mo Pro -> $100/mo Max. macOS Sonoma 14.2+ only.
Why it wins attention: PLACEMENT — help appears beside the cursor at the moment of
friction, not in a chat window. Praised onboarding. "10x of screenshotting into Claude."
Weaknesses vs us: session-based helper, not an always-on life OS. No persistent identity
or memory of YOUR life. No wake word/room presence. No business-automation hands (n8n).
Cloud product = their servers see your screen; platform risk vs Apple Intelligence.

## 2. Luke Cutting / Azaris AI (lukebuildsai, 244K IG)
What it is: personal J.A.R.V.I.S. running his business — a manager-agent delegating to
specialist agents. Flagship proof: a content agent that pulls footage, edits, and posts
across platforms daily for a month; claims 3x app growth. Monetized via free 5-day email
crash course -> Skool community ("Agentic AI for Founders") -> azaris.ai product.
Why it wins attention: PROOF OF WORK content ("How did he build this?" 67K views) +
critique hook ("most AI agents are fancy UI with nothing behind them").
Weaknesses vs us: business-ops focused, no voice-first room presence, no vision/screen
recall, persona is branding not a behavioral system. It's a funnel as much as a product.

## What to STEAL (adopt into Friday)
1. HeyClicky's capture ethic: screen context ON DEMAND is a great privacy story — we
   already better it (screenpipe local-only), but SAY it like they do.
2. On-screen pointing: post-v1 candidate — Friday draws an arrow/highlight when answering
   "where is X on my screen?" (add to Phase 7+ backlog).
3. Luke's manager->specialist agent pattern: our ops node can evolve into a delegator
   over n8n specialist flows. Also: proof-of-work demo content beats feature lists.
4. Friction placement: our equivalent of "beside the cursor" is "already in the room" —
   the wake phrase IS our placement. Lean on it in demos.
